# Novelty Boundary Map

## Crowded Territory

- Generic hierarchical reinforcement learning.
- Option termination alone.
- Safety filters that halt low-level actions.
- Retry-based recovery policies.
- Failure-aware task planning without containment-level diagnosis.

## Claimed Boundary

Hierarchical failure containment models where a failure should be stopped or repaired in the control hierarchy before it cascades into task-state corruption.

## What Would Falsify The Claim

If failure-aware hierarchical controllers, option termination monitors, safety filters, or reactive retry policies match proposed success, containment, cascade, corruption, damage, and false-halt rates under combined cascade stress, the paper should be killed or reframed.
